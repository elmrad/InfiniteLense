ABOUT MORE IN YOU

Education should not stop at the classroom door.

We help educators turn the places around them into safe, purposeful and measurable learning experiences.

THE PROBLEM

Curriculum and place are disconnected.

Teachers have limited time to translate formal outcomes into safe outdoor experiences. Schools also underuse the assets, spaces and communities already around them.

OUR APPROACH

Curriculum first. Place aware. Teacher controlled.

The portal connects authorised standards to observable environments, proposes feasible activities and captures evidence—while educators approve every consequential decision.
